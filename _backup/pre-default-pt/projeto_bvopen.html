2020

Banco BV

The BV Open website project represents a significant milestone in providing information about Banco BV's APIs. This website was created to guide developers and partners on how to use the APIs in different digital ecosystems, as well as to provide insights on becoming a BV partner and leveraging the BV Open API management platform.

Job tags:

UX Design
Workshops

The Challenge

The website was developed in collaboration with the BV Open team after two days of meetings that materialized the business needs and expectations. A structure and content validation workshop was also conducted to ensure the website met the requirements. The BV Open website is a vital resource for those who wish to explore the possibilities offered by Banco BV's APIs. It provides detailed documentation, practical examples, and step-by-step guides to assist developers in integrating the APIs into their projects. Additionally, the website offers information about the benefits of becoming a BV partner, including access to exclusive resources and specialized support. One of the key features of the website is the BV Open API management platform, which allows developers to explore and test the API functionalities interactively. Through this platform, users can create and manage their own applications, conduct tests, and gain valuable insights to enhance their solutions.

Learnings

In summary, the BV Open website project signifies Banco BV's commitment to transparency, support, and advanced resources for developers and partners interested in utilizing the APIs. Through collaborative creation and a focus on excellence, the website has become a trusted source of information and essential tools to drive innovation and growth in the digital ecosystem.
