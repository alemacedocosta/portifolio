Short resume

In the last 20 years working as a designer, I became a specialist in digital editorial (for tablets), then I got to know Design thinking that changed my way of looking at and doing design, I worked as a Brand designer for a big company and finally became a UX designer when I could help the São Paulo's Education Department, Dock Means of Payments and BV Bank to create amazing experiences. Today, AI is part of my daily work, helping me explore ideas faster and build better products.

How I work

Time capsule

Here, I talk a little about each period of my journey as a designer, from the beginning when I worked more as a graphic designer to the present moment, after transitioning to product design. In a summarized way, I will share about each phase and, within each phase, the activities I carried out.

2022/2026

Now, back at Banco BV, I am helping to develop a Backoffice product for the bank's Pricing area where the entire product logic has been highly altered to give more flexibility and new possibilities to the depricing structuring team. Another backoffice experience that I love to do. As before, I bring the necessary product vision to the team together with PO and PM, at the same time that I bring the solutions for each case. A work done from the beginning through the research, through the definition, ideation, prototyping and testing and finally, the delivery.
