2018

Secretaria Municipal de Educação de São Paulo

During a year and two months, I had the opportunity to be part of a large-scale project as a UX designer for the Municipal Education Secretariat of São Paulo. The project involved the development of a Pedagogical Management System (SGP) for the entire municipal school network.

The Challenge

As a member of the design team, I played a key role in creating an intuitive and efficient user experience for the teachers who would be using the system. One of our main priorities was to ensure that the SGP met the real needs of the users, the teachers. Therefore, we frequently conducted validations with real users through usability tests and discovery workshops. These validation sessions were crucial in understanding the demands, expectations, and workflows of the teachers. Through usability testing, we were able to identify pain points and areas for improvement in the system, allowing us to make continuous adjustments and enhancements.

Additionally, an important part of my role as a UX designer was managing the Design System of the SGP. I worked in collaboration with the developers to ensure that the system's components were up-to-date and aligned with the established design guidelines. This helped maintain visual consistency and interface standardization across all system functionalities. I also actively participated in refining each created functionality. Working closely with the developers, we analyzed interactions and workflows to optimize the usability and efficiency of the system. My contribution involved proposing design solutions that considered both the user experience and technical constraints.

Learnings

It was a rewarding experience to be part of such a significant project with social impact. Through my role as a UX designer in the development of the SGP system, I was able to contribute to the improvement of pedagogical management for teachers in the municipal school network of São Paulo, providing them with an effective and intuitive tool for their daily work.
