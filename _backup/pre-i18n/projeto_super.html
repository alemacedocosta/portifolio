2012

Ed. Abril

In 2012, I had the incredible opportunity to create an entire Superinteressante magazine for the iPad. It was a thrilling experience that allowed me to explore the realm of digital publishing and push the boundaries of storytelling. Being able to harness the interactive capabilities of the iPad opened up a whole new world of creativity and engagement. With this project, I could dive into the realm of multimedia content, seamlessly integrating videos, animations, and audio elements to enhance the reader's experience. The tablet technology enabled me to craft immersive narratives and present information in dynamic and captivating ways. From pop-ups to transition animations and parallax effects, every feature aimed to captivate and educate the audience.

The Challenge

The creation of the entire edition of Super Interessante magazine for tablets, with the theme of creativity, was an incredible and challenging experience. The possibility of creating an interactive and immersive reading experience using tablet technology allowed us to explore new ways of telling stories and presenting information in a more dynamic and engaging way.


One of the main aspects of tablet interactivity is the ability to create multimedia content, such as videos, animations, and audio, that complement and enrich the reading experience. In addition, it is possible to create interactive features, such as pop-ups, transition animations, parallax effects, among others, that help make the content more dynamic and interesting.



For the edition creation, we used Adobe Publishing Suite technology, which allows the creation of tablet content with ease and efficiency. With this tool, it was possible to create responsive layouts, optimize images, and manage content in an intuitive and organized way.


Another highlight of the edition was the interactive infographics, which allowed the exploration of data in a more dynamic and engaging way. We used resources such as interactive maps, animated graphics, and layered infographics to present information clearly and objectively.



Learnings

In summary, the creation of the Super Interessante edition for tablets was a rich and challenging experience, which allowed us to explore new ways of telling stories and presenting information in a more engaging and interactive way. The use of Adobe Publishing Suite technology and interactive infographics were crucial to the success of the edition, and the partnership with Editora Abril contributed to the excellence of the project as a whole.

Editora Abril was an important partner in this project, providing support and resources to ensure the quality of the content and delivery of the edition on time. It was an honor to work with such a renowned and recognized company in the publishing market.
