Escola Aqui app

[ Scroll page for more ]

2019

Secretaria municipal de Educação de São Paulo

This was a very interesting project aimed at supporting parents and teachers across the entire municipal school network of São Paulo. Through the app, it provided information on students' grades and attendance throughout the year. This application impacted more than 120,000 families and 40,000 teachers.
